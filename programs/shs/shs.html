Senior High School

Perpetual Help College of Manila (PHCM) Senior High School is committed to providing quality education that shapes students into future leaders and responsible citizens. Aligned with the K to 12 Curriculum, PHCM offers a dynamic and holistic learning experience that fosters academic excellence, critical thinking, and leadership. Guided by the belief that character building is nation-building, PHCM instills Perpetualite values of faith, integrity, and service, empowering students to contribute meaningfully to society and the nation’s progress.

LEARN MORE ➜

Vision

The UPHSL Senior High School aims tom produce Christian leaders research-oriented and competent graduates who have mastered the necessary concepts needed for tertiary education and have developed the skills for employment and entrepreneurship.

Mission

The UPHSL Senior High School will be a benchmark of excellence in delivering quality education and in inculcating Christian values leading to its graduates towards the attainment of the best quality of life.

Senior High School

Program Educational Objectives (PEOs)

Guided by the University’s Mission, the graduates of Senior High School are/can:


  • Possess appropriate knowledge, skills, and attitudes for the world of work.
  • Practice entrepreneurship, research, and technical competencies that will enable them to dynamically respond to varying societal conditions.
  • Demonstrate proficiency in oral and written communication for effective human relations.
  • Demonstrate integral character and moral values in their personal lives and interpersonal dealings.
  • Manifest Christian leadership skills through community service and outreach activities

Program Outcomes

  • Exhibit a comprehensive understanding of how the different competencies relate to the larger historical, religious, scientific moral, socio-cultural and political processes.
  • Reflect in his lifestyle and everyday life a deep and principled understanding of his lineage as a Filipino and a Christian.
  • Use communication as a tool to promote Filipino and Christian values, gain competencies, enhance his unique history as a people, serve his community and society and lead others in accomplishing meaningful human activities.
  • Practice a value system that is grounded on Christian teachings and Filipino cultural values and upright moral beliefs.
  • Exhibit critical and analysis skills in decision making.
  • Exhibit competencies that will enable him to respond to the requirements and expectations of his chosen course, lead community outreach initiatives, promote his Filipino culture and Christian beliefs and venture into entrepreneurial and other livelihood related undertakings.
  • Employ creative and innovative approaches using required competencies in diverse types of learning in wide ranges of situations, in community service, in their personal everyday life and in heeding to their patriotic duties.

    Career Paths

    STEM (Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics)

    • For the past years, the department has produced a number of Science enthusiasts! With our competitive Science and Health-related programs on the campus, it is assured that our students are prepared to be STEM professionals!
    • Possible courses related: BS Biology, BS Medical Technology, BS Psychology, BS Radiologic Technology, BS Computer Sciences, BS Agriculture, Engineering courses, Medicine and Health-related courses (etc.)

    ABM (Accounting, Business, and Management)

    • Through the established program of our institution for business courses, our department was able to hone competitive and business-minded problem solvers in this track! Indeed, through this, great numbers come with great analysts.
    • Possible courses related: BS Business Administration, BS Entrepreneurship, BS Marketing, BS Accountancy, BS Banking and Finance, BS Economics, business and management related courses (etc.)

    HUMSS (Humanities and Social Sciences)

    • Thinkers and creative doers, they have been at the core of the department's foundation. Through discussions, debates, and critical thinking activities, the department has produced individuals who can present themselves in the public.
    • Possible courses related: BS and AB Psychology, AB Political Science, AB Mass Communication, AB Creative writing, AB Sociology, AB Economics, Education courses, arts-related courses (etc.)

    TVL (Technical and Vocational Livelihood)
    • With knowledge and wit, the department brings out a creative combination as these join with skills. This track has been a consistent path to skillful learning. With the available resources provided by the institution, the department has proudly cultivated great practice among the students.
    • Possible courses related: BS Tourism, BS HRM, BS Travel Management, BS Computer Science, BS, Information systems, BS Information Technology, Computer and Culinary-related courses (etc.)
